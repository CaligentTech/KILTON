- ABOUT US
INTEGRATED TECHNOLOGY SOLUTIONS

Technology Expertise. Business Understanding.

Kilton Technologies is an integrated ICT and technology solutions company serving businesses and projects across the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia.

Our capabilities span enterprise networking, IT infrastructure, wireless connectivity, security and surveillance, access control, AV and unified communications, fire alarm systems, enterprise hardware, software, storage, backup and cybersecurity.

We believe technology should be selected based on the problem it needs to solve—not simply on specifications.

A network project is more than installing switches. It requires consideration of bandwidth, architecture, security, redundancy, PoE requirements, and future expansion.

A CCTV project is more than installing cameras. Coverage, image quality, storage, network capacity, monitoring, and recording duration all matter.

A conference room is more than mounting a display. Cameras, microphones, speakers, collaboration platforms, connectivity, and user experience must work together.

OUR APPROACH Understand the requirement, identify the right solution, integrate the components, and support the customer throughout the process.
